Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026 · Quantumy AI

This Privacy Policy explains how Quantumy AI (“we”, “the Service”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application at quantumy.work.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data — email address and authentication details when you sign up.
  • Conversation data — messages you send and responses generated by the AI.
  • Connected services (optional) — if you connect Gmail, we store OAuth tokens and the account email so the Service can access only the data you authorize (read-only Gmail).
  • Technical data — basic logs needed to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the AI chatbot and conversation history.
  • To call AI providers (e.g. Anthropic) with your prompts so they can generate responses.
  • To access connected sources (e.g. Gmail) only when you ask and only with the permissions you granted.
  • To maintain security and reliability.

3. Connected accounts (Gmail)

Connectors are optional. When you connect Gmail, we request read-only access so Quantumy AI can search and summarize messages you ask about. We do not sell your email content. You can disconnect in Settings → Connectors, which removes stored tokens.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication and database.
  • Anthropic — AI model responses.
  • Google — only if you connect Gmail, under Google’s terms and the scopes you approve.
  • Vercel — hosting.

5. Data retention & your choices

Account and chat data remain while your account is active. You may delete chats in the app, disconnect connectors, or stop using the Service at any time.

6. Contact

Questions: use the developer/support email listed on the Quantumy AI Google OAuth consent screen.
