Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026 · Quantumy AI

This Privacy Policy explains how Quantumy AI (“we”, “the Service”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application at https://quantumy.work.

Limited use of Google user data. Quantumy AI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only use Gmail data to provide features you request in the product (search and summarize your own messages). We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data — email address and authentication details when you sign up.
  • Conversation data — messages you send and responses generated by the AI.
  • Connected services (optional) — if you connect Gmail, we store OAuth tokens and the connected account email so the Service can access only the data you authorize (read-only Gmail).
  • Technical data — basic logs needed to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the AI chatbot and conversation history.
  • To call AI providers (e.g. Anthropic) with your prompts so they can generate responses.
  • To access connected sources (e.g. Gmail) only when you ask and only with the permissions you granted.
  • To maintain security and reliability.

3. Google / Gmail access (optional)

Connecting Gmail is optional. When you connect, Quantumy AI requests:

  • gmail.readonly — read-only access to search and summarize messages you ask about.
  • userinfo.email — to identify the Google account you connected.

We do not request permission to send, delete, or modify your email. We do not sell your email content. You can disconnect in the app under Settings → Connectors, which removes stored tokens, or revoke access in your Google Account settings.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication and database.
  • Anthropic — AI model responses.
  • Google — only if you connect Gmail, under Google’s terms and the scopes you approve.
  • Vercel — hosting.

5. Data retention & your choices

Account and chat data remain while your account is active. You may delete chats in the app, disconnect connectors, or stop using the Service at any time. Upon request we will assist with account-related data deletion where technically feasible.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy or Google data use: contact the developer support email listed on the Quantumy AI Google OAuth consent screen, or visit https://quantumy.work/home.html.
