Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026 · Quantum AI

This Privacy Policy explains how Quantum AI (“we”, “the Service”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application at quantumy-xi.vercel.app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data — email address and authentication details when you sign up (via Supabase Auth).
  • Conversation data — messages you send and responses generated by the AI, stored so you can access chat history.
  • Connected services (optional) — if you connect Gmail or other connectors, we store OAuth tokens and the account email so the Service can access only the data you authorize (e.g. read-only Gmail).
  • Technical data — basic logs needed to operate and secure the Service (e.g. errors, request metadata).

2. How we use information

  • To provide the AI chatbot and conversation history.
  • To call third-party AI providers (e.g. Anthropic) with your prompts so they can generate responses.
  • To access connected sources (e.g. Gmail) only when you ask and only with the permissions you granted.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

3. Connected accounts (Gmail and others)

Connectors are optional and granular. When you connect Gmail, we request read-only access so Quantum can search and summarize messages you ask about. We do not sell your email content. You can disconnect a connector at any time in Settings → Connectors, which removes stored tokens.

4. Third-party services

We use trusted processors to run the Service, including:

  • Supabase — authentication and database.
  • Anthropic — AI model responses.
  • Google — only if you connect a Google service (e.g. Gmail), under Google’s terms and the scopes you approve.
  • Vercel — hosting.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies as needed to provide their services.

5. Data retention

Account and conversation data are kept while your account is active. You may delete conversations in the app. Disconnecting a connector deletes stored OAuth tokens for that connector. You may request account deletion by contacting us using the email below.

6. Security

We use industry-standard practices such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), server-side API keys, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your choices

  • Disconnect connectors anytime in Settings.
  • Delete individual chats from the sidebar.
  • Sign out or stop using the Service at any time.

8. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen for this app, or through your Quantum account email channel.
