Purpose of this application

Quantumy AI is a productivity app whose purpose is to give you a personal AI chatbot with persistent memory, help drafting clear emails, and optional read-only Gmail so you can ask about your own inbox in plain language. Connectors are opt-in and can be disconnected anytime. Everything is designed so the assistant stays useful across sessions — without forcing a long setup flow.
