Quantumy AI — your personal work assistant

Purpose of this app: Quantumy AI is a web application that lets you chat with an AI assistant about your work. You can optionally connect your Gmail account (read-only) so the assistant can search and summarize your email when you ask. Connecting Google is optional and controlled by you.

Open the app Privacy Policy

What Quantumy AI does

  • Sign in with email to use a private AI chat workspace.
  • Ask questions in everyday language and get clear answers.
  • Optionally connect Gmail so the AI can find and summarize messages you request.
  • Disconnect Gmail anytime from Settings → Connectors.

How Google access is used

If you choose to connect Gmail, Quantumy AI requests read-only access so it can search your mailbox when you ask (for example: “summarize unread emails from this week”). We do not use Gmail data for advertising. Details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Features

Private chat

Conversations are saved to your account so you can continue later.

Optional Gmail connector

Connect only if you want email search. One source at a time, clear permissions.

Your control

Disconnect anytime. You decide what the assistant can access.
